# Assets and licences

Every shipped asset is either generated by this repository's scripts or derived from a **CC0** source. CC0 needs
no attribution; Poly Haven is credited here anyway.

**Since Phase 1 (2026-11-18) no photographic texture ships.** Every surface (ground, rock, stone, moss, bark) is a
painted material in the art bible's palette, built in Blender (`painted_shader` in `scripts/blender/common.py`)
and baked. The two Poly Haven skies remain, for lighting and the sky panorama only.

## External sources (all Poly Haven, CC0 — https://polyhaven.com/license)

Downloaded by `npm run assets:fetch` (`scripts/fetch_polyhaven.py`) at 2K into `assets/source-textures/polyhaven/`
(gitignored, re-downloadable). Approved limit: 2 HDRIs and 8 material sets.

| Poly Haven ID | Type | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` | HDRI | Modern HD's sky: image-based lighting (512×256 HDR), panorama, sun direction |
| `kloppenheim_06_puresky` | HDRI | Beyond's golden-hour sky, rotated so the low sun sits in the west and graded warm |
| `rock_face_03`, `mossy_rock`, `rough_block_wall`, `bark_brown_02`, `leafy_grass`, `forest_leaves_02`, `dirt_floor`, `forrest_ground_01` | Materials | Used until Phase 1; **no longer shipped** (replaced by the painted materials). `scripts/fetch_polyhaven.py` still fetches them; nothing reads them |

Tree foliage is an original drawing (`scripts/textures/leaf-atlas.mjs`).
